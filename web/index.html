💡 Professor Viva Voce Lab Examination High-Frequency Questions

Q1: What is the convoy effect in CPU scheduling, and which algorithm eliminates it?

The convoy effect occurs in non-preemptive FCFS when a long CPU-bound process blocks smaller I/O-bound processes. It is resolved by preemptive algorithms like Round Robin or SRTF which enforce maximum time slices.

Q2: Why does Belady's Anomaly occur in FIFO but never in LRU or Optimal?

LRU and Optimal belong to the class of stack algorithms that satisfy the inclusion property (pages in n frames are always a subset of n+1 frames). FIFO does not satisfy inclusion, allowing fault counts to increase as physical frame allocation grows.

Q3: Is an unsafe state in Banker's algorithm synonymous with a deadlocked state?

No. An unsafe state is not necessarily deadlocked; it is a state from which deadlock cannot be guaranteed to be avoided if processes simultaneously demand their maximum declared resource claims.

Q4: Why does LOOK scheduling generally outperform SCAN?

SCAN unconditionally travels all the way to the physical cylinder edge (track 199 or 0), even when no requests exist at the boundary. LOOK reverses direction immediately at the furthest pending request, cutting down needless mechanical seek time.
